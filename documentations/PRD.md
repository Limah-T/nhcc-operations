## Problem Statement

The Nigerian-Hungarian Chamber of Commerce (NHCC) managed its operational records manually using spreadsheets and Microsoft Word. Administrative tasks such as expense tracking, report generation, staff and member onboarding, activity tracking, and document preparation relied on repetitive manual processes.

This approach resulted in inaccurate calculations, duplicate or misplaced records, time-consuming report preparation, and delayed access to operational information. As a result, management often had to wait for the administrative department to prepare and deliver the latest reports before making decisions.

## Features

* Staff Management – Onboard, update, and manage staff records.
* Salary Management – Record and track staff salaries and bonuses.
* Diesel Management – Record and monitor diesel expenses.
* Electricity Management – Record and monitor electricity (EKEDC) expenses.
* Office Expense Management – Record and categorize office expenses.
* Reports – Generate operational and financial reports.
* PDF Export – Export reports as downloadable PDF documents.
* Authentication & Authorization – Secure user access based on permissions and record user activities.
* Dashboard Overview provides quick access to:
    * Current Diesel Expenditure
    * Current Month Electricity
    * Current Office Expenses
    * Current Year Expenditure
    * Recent Activity Log
    * Upcoming Events
    * Announcements

## Success Criteria
* Manual administrative work is significantly reduced.
* Expense calculations are accurate and consistent.
* Reports are generated within seconds.
* Staff records can be created, updated, and retrieved efficiently.
* Operational records are centrally stored and easily accessible.
* Authorized users can access dashboard summaries immediately after login.
* User activities are recorded for accountability.


## Constraints
- The application is deployed on the free tier of PythonAnywhere.
- The free hosting environment has limited processing power and concurrent connections.
- Performance may degrade when many users access the application simultaneously.
- SQLite is currently used for data storage.
